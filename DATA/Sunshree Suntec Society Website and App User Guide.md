# Sunshree Suntec Society Website and App: User Guide

Nov 16, 2026 · @Chanchal Sakarde for www.sunshreesuntec.com

## 1. About this guide

This guide explains, in plain words, how members, staff and the managing committee of Sunshree Suntec Co-op. Housing Society use the Society's website and Android app. It covers software version 3.8.0 (shown at the foot of the accounts page).

You don't need to read it all. Find yourself in the table and read those sections.

| You are | Read |
| --- | --- |
| A flat or shop owner, family member or tenant | 2, 3, 4, 15, 16 |
| Watchman, housekeeping staff | 3, 5, 12 (Add expense), 13, 15 |
| Society manager, office assistant, accountant | 3, 5, 6 and the sections for the tabs you use |
| Auditor or government official (e.g. DDR office) | 3, 5, 6, 10, 12 |
| Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, committee member | All of it |

Some words have a special meaning here, such as *unit*, *voucher* or *payback*. Section 17 explains them. Buttons and tab names are written in **bold**, exactly as they appear on screen.

## 2. The website and the app at a glance

The system has three parts. Anyone can see the public pages; the other two need a sign-in that the committee has approved.

| Part | Where | Who uses it | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public website | sunshreesuntec.com | Everyone | Home, About, Maintenance dues, Notices, Committee, Contact |
| Member area | Website → **Member login** | Approved owners, family, tenants, caretakers | Your unit's balance, bills, receipts and account entries; the Society's accounts and documents |
| Society accounts page | Website → **Member login** → **Open Society accounts** (committee and staff) | Committee, staff, auditors, officials | The tabs each person is allowed: bills, payments, units, expenses, members and more (section 6) |

The Android app shows the same three parts, and keeps some pages on the phone so they can be read without internet (section 15).

### The maintenance dues page

The **Maintenance dues** page lists every unit by wing and floor, with the member's name. You can search by name or unit number.

- **Amounts are hidden from visitors.** Each digit shows as an x, so ₹ 1,17,500 appears as *x,xx,xxx*. This keeps amounts private while showing roughly how large a due is.
- **To see the real amounts,** tap the small **eye** button or **Show amounts**. Approved members, committee and staff see them at once. Anyone else is taken to **Member login** and brought back after signing in.
- **"Cr"** after an amount means the member has paid in advance.
- **"Under verification"** means the old (opening) balance of that unit is still being checked by the committee.

The page updates by itself whenever a bill, payment or correction is recorded.

## 3. Registering and signing in

Everyone registers once on the website's **Member login** page, and the committee approves each request before any information is shown. Each person uses their own email: never share a login.

### Two ways to sign in

- **Continue with Google** (easiest): uses your Gmail account, no new password. In the app it uses the Google account already on the phone.
- **Email and password**: choose **Register**, enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters, then open the confirmation link sent to your email. Until the email is confirmed, amounts stay hidden.

After you tap a sign-in button, a message under it says what is happening (*Opening Google sign-in…*, *Signing you in…*). Please wait for it; tapping again does nothing until the first try finishes.

### Registering as a member of a unit

1. Open **Member login** and choose **Continue with Google**, or **Register** with email and password.
2. Under **I am registering as**, choose **A member of a unit**.
3. Fill in your **Full name**, your **Unit / flat / shop number**, **You are** (Owner, Joint owner, Family member of the owner, Tenant, or Caretaker / agent of the owner) and your **Mobile number**.
4. Tick that the details are true and press **Send request**.
5. The page now shows *Request sent*. The committee checks it against the Society's member register, usually within a few days.

Several people can register for the same unit, each with their own email (for example husband, wife and a tenant). One login belongs to one unit only; someone with two units registers the second one with a different email.

### Registering as staff or an official

Managers, accountants, housekeeping staff, watchmen, auditors and government officials (for example the Deputy Registrar's office) use the same page:

1. Under **I am registering as**, choose **Society staff or an official**.
2. Fill in your **Full name**, **Role**, **Organisation or office** (optional, e.g. the audit firm) and **Mobile number**. The form shows what that role usually gets.
3. Tick that you will use the access only for the Society's work and keep members' information confidential, then press **Send request**.
4. Once the committee approves, open the Society accounts page and sign in with the same email. Most staff must first read and accept the staff agreement (section 5).

### What happens after you send a request

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request sent / waiting for approval | The committee hasn't decided yet | Wait; you may be told by email or WhatsApp |
| Your account page opens | Approved | Nothing: you're in |
| Request not approved, with a reason | Rejected | Contact the Society office if it's a mistake |
| Access removed, with a reason | The committee ended your access (e.g. flat sold) | Contact the Society office if needed |
| Your access ended on (date) | Time-limited access (auditors, officials) has run out | Ask the committee to extend it |

### Forgot your password

On **Member login** choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email and press the button. A link to set a new password arrives by email. People who use **Continue with Google** have no Society password; they use their Google account.

### Signing out

Press **Sign out** at the top of the page. On a shared phone or computer, always sign out. In the app, signing out also removes the pages and files saved on the phone.

## 4. For members: your account

Once approved, **Member login** opens your unit's account. The top shows your unit, your name and who you are (e.g. Owner), then your balance.

### What you will find

| Part | What it shows | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summary | Amount due today, unpaid interest, and any old builder-period dues (BPM) shown separately | "Cr" means you have paid in advance |
| Bills | Every monthly bill: number, month, due date, total | **Open** a bill to see each charge and how it was worked out; you can print it or save it as PDF |
| Receipts | Every payment recorded: receipt number, date, mode (cash, cheque, UPI, bank), amount | **Open** to print or save the receipt |
| Balance at the end of each financial year | Your balance on 31 March of each year, from the audited accounts (up to 2017-18) and then the Society's books | Useful when comparing with your own records |
| Account entries | Every entry since 2011: bills, payments, corrections, with a running balance | If a payment of yours is missing, show the receipt to the committee |
| Society accounts | The Society's audited accounts year by year: income, expenditure, surplus, members' dues | Choose the **Financial year** to see that year |
| Society documents | Audit reports, AGM minutes and other papers the committee has put up | **Open** to read or download |

### How your bill is worked out

Each line of a bill has a note explaining it, for example *Equal to all 65 units* or *10% of service charges*. The bottom line names the General Body resolution or bye-law used. Lift charges appear only for flats served by a lift, and are shared only among them.

### Paying your dues

Pay by the Society's usual methods (cheque, bank transfer, UPI or cash at the office). The payment appears in **Receipts** once the office records it, and the dues page updates at the same moment. Interest, where the General Body has fixed it, is simple interest on unpaid amounts only, never interest on interest.

### Tenants and caretakers

Tenants and caretakers usually see only the Society's accounts and documents, not the unit's bills or balance, because those belong to the owner. The page tells you this. If you pay the maintenance for the owner, ask the committee to give you access to the unit account; they do this with the owner's consent.

### Something looks wrong?

Write to the committee or visit the office with the bill or receipt number. Every change to an account is recorded with who made it and when, and nothing is ever deleted, so any entry can be traced.

## 5. Who can do what: access policy

Each person sees only what their role needs, and the system itself enforces it: a button that isn't yours doesn't appear, and the database refuses the action even if someone tries another way.

### Members of a unit

| Who | Can see | Set by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner, joint owner, family member | Unit account (bills, receipts, balance, entries) and Society accounts and documents | Suggested on approval |
| Tenant, caretaker / agent | Society accounts and documents only | Suggested on approval; the committee can give the unit account too, with the owner's consent |

Members never see other units' details, apart from the names and amounts on the dues page.

### Committee

The Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer and other managing committee members can do everything. Some things only the committee can do, and they can't be given to staff:

- change rates, charge basis and Society or bank details (**Settings**);
- import opening balances;
- add, change or remove staff and their access;
- edit and publish the staff agreement.

### Rules that always apply

- **Nobody approves their own expense.** A Chairman's expense is approved by the Secretary or Treasurer, and so on.
- **Access with an end date stops by itself** on that date (auditors, officials). The committee can extend it.
- **Suspended or removed access** stops at once.
- **Every action is recorded** with the person's name and time. Bills, payments, receipts, decisions and their history are never deleted; mistakes are corrected with a new entry and a reason.
- **Staff who have the residents directory** can see approved residents' names, relation and mobile numbers, and nothing about money.

### The staff agreement

Before their first piece of work, most staff see the *Commitment, Confidentiality and Work Responsibilities Agreement*. They read it, type their name and accept. Until then nothing else opens. In short, it says:

- use the access only for the Society's work and only with your own login;
- keep members' names, phone numbers, balances and documents confidential, also after leaving;
- make every entry true, on the day it happens, backed by a document; never alter or back-date an entry;
- deposit cash and cheques by the next working day;
- declare any personal interest in a matter, and report mistakes or misuse at once;
- hand over all records when leaving.

It also lists duties for the role (for example the Accountant reconciles receipts with the bank statement every month). When the committee publishes a new version, staff accept it again at their next sign-in. Auditors and government officials don't need to accept it.

## 7. Dashboard, Bills and Payments

These three tabs cover the monthly cycle: bills go out on the 1st, payments come in and are receipted, and the Dashboard shows where things stand.

### Dashboard

Shows, as on today: **Total outstanding**, **Advances / credits**, the amount **Billed** this month and the amount **Collected** this month, then the units with the **Largest outstanding** balances, for follow-up.

### Bills

Bills are made automatically on the 1st of every month, one per unit. The committee or Accountant only acts for special cases.

- **To see bills:** choose the month under **Show month**. Each row shows bill number, unit, name, arrears, this month's charges and total; open one to see every line.
- **Bill number** looks like *C402/01112026* (unit, then bill date).
- **To make bills by hand** (if the automatic run was missed): choose **Month**, **Bill date** and **Due date**, then **Generate bills**. Running it again is safe: only missing bills are added.
- **One-off charge for many units** (e.g. a festival contribution or a major repairs fund): fill **Extra one-off charge**, **Extra amount**, **How to charge the extra** (same amount for each unit, a total divided equally, or a total divided by carpet area) and **Apply extra to** (all units or one wing), then generate. A penalty above ₹5,000 per member is refused, as the rules require.

What a bill contains, in order: maintenance / service charges, sinking fund, repairs fund, non-occupancy charges (let-out flats), arrears, interest on arrears (only if the General Body set a rate), water, lift charges, parking, then one-off items such as transfer fee or cheque return charges. Optional lines, such as *Lift AMC / Repairs (shared)*, appear only when used.

### Payments

Use **Payments** each time money is received from a member.

1. Choose the **Unit**.
2. Enter **Payment date**, **Amount**, **Mode** (NEFT, IMPS, UPI, Cheque, Cash, Demand Draft) and the **UTR / cheque / ref. no.** (needed for everything except cash). For cheques, add the **Bank**.
3. Optionally add **Against bill no.** and a **Note**.
4. Press **Save payment and create receipt**.

The receipt gets the next number for the year (e.g. *SS/2026-27/0001*, starting again each 1 April), the unit's balance drops, and the dues page updates, all at the same moment. The member can then see and print the receipt.

A payment first clears any unpaid interest, then the rest of the dues.

- **Receipt register:** choose a **Month** to list all receipts; **Download CSV** gives the list for Excel or the auditor.
- **Cancelling a receipt** (bounced cheque, wrong entry): press **Cancel** on its row in the register and give a reason. The receipt stays in the register marked *Cancelled*, and the amount goes back on the unit's account. Receipts are never deleted. Needs the *Cancel receipts* permission.

### Corrections to a unit's account

If an amount must be added to or taken off a unit (a verified old balance, a waiver approved by the committee, an entry made on the wrong unit), open the unit (**Units** → **Open**) and use **Add a correction**: debit adds to dues, credit reduces them. Always write the reason and the approval in the particulars. Needs the *Add corrections* permission.

## 8. Units and the Unit file

The **Units** tab lists all flats and shops; the **Unit file** holds everything about one unit in one place.

### Units

- The list shows each unit's number, wing, owner's name, category, lift and status. **Open** a unit to see or change its details.
- **Unit details** (with *Edit unit details*): member / owner name, premises type, charge category, **Status** (Own occupied, Rented, Tenant: rented units pay non-occupancy charges), **Served by lift** (C wing, D wing or none), carpet area, water usage, two-wheelers and four-wheelers for parking. Balances can't be changed here; use a correction (section 7).
- **Enter area, water usage and lift for all units** opens a grid to fill these for every unit at once. **Fill carpet areas from Index II** and **Fill shops from approved plan** fill carpet areas from the registered documents; check them, then **Save all units**.
- **Add unit** (committee): a new unit starts at ₹0 and is marked *Under verification* until its opening balance is added.

### Unit file

Open it from **Units**, or choose a unit in **Go to unit**. It has four parts:

| Part | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Balance, unpaid interest, old builder-period dues (BPM), occupancy, last bill, and every entry, bill and receipt, including past years |
| **Members (I & J register)** | Present and past members: date of admission, entrance fee, share certificate and share numbers, I and J register serial numbers, nomination, and when membership ended |
| **Tenants and rental history** | Each tenancy: tenant, period, leave and licence agreement, police verification, Society NOC and links to the documents. Warnings show expired agreements and missing police verification. The unit's status (Rented / Own occupied) can be changed here |
| **Communication** | Every letter, notice, email, message, call or meeting with the unit, with a reference and a follow-up date |

Adding or changing members, tenants and communication needs *Maintain unit files*. Documents are kept as links (for example to the Society's Google Drive). **Print unit file** prints all four parts. Records are never deleted, except by the committee.

## 9. Lift expenses

Lift costs are paid only by the flats each lift serves: the C wing lift by C wing flats, the D wing lift by D wing flats. Shops and A wing have no lift and pay nothing for lifts.

There are two kinds of lift charge on bills:

- **Lift Charges**: the regular yearly (or monthly) lift charge fixed by the General Body.
- **Lift AMC / Repairs (shared)**: a particular cost, such as an AMC invoice, a repair, parts or a licence, divided equally among the flats served by that lift. Recorded in the **Lift** tab.

Don't recover the same cost both ways. If the yearly lift charge already pays for the AMC, don't also share the AMC.

### Recording a lift expense (Lift tab)

1. Enter the **Date of expense**, choose the **Lift** (C wing, D wing, or both lifts on one contract) and the **Type** (AMC, Repair, Wear & tear / parts, Licence / inspection, Electricity, Other).
2. Write a **Description** (e.g. *AMC 1 Oct 2026 to 30 Sep 2027*), the **Vendor**, **Invoice no.** and **Paid by** (cheque / UTR), and the **Amount**.
3. Choose **Recover from bill month** and the **Number of monthly instalments** (1 to 24).
4. Check the line under the form: it shows how many flats share it and each flat's share, per month if spread out.
5. Press **Save lift expense** and confirm.

The bill month must be one not yet billed. Each affected bill shows the description, invoice, total, number of flats and the share.

The **Lift expense register** lists every lift expense with its share per flat and which bills include it, and the total for this financial year per lift. An entry can be deleted only before it appears on a bill.

### From an approved expense

A lift cost entered under **Expenses** (for example a vendor's AMC contract with its photo) can be sent to the members' bills from that expense, once approved: see *Lift costs recovered from members* in section 12. It then appears in this register too, with its voucher number; tapping the voucher opens the expense and its bill photo.

## 10. Reports and Documents

Reports are worked out by financial year (1 April to 31 March), from the units' accounts, bills and receipts. Any past year can be reported at any time.

### Reports

Choose the **Financial year**, then one of the reports. Every report can be printed (**Print**, or save as PDF) or downloaded for Excel (**Download CSV**).

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Year summary** | Opening dues, billed, received, corrections, closing dues, collection percentage, month by month |
| **Audited statements** | For 2011-12 to 2017-18: the figures from that year's audit report |
| **Over the years** | Every year from 2011-12 to today side by side: income, expenditure, surplus, members' dues |
| **Member-wise statement** | For each unit: opening on 1 April, billed, received, cancelled receipts, corrections, closing on 31 March |
| **Billing by head** | Total billed in the year under each bill line (maintenance, water, lift and so on) |
| **Collections** | Amounts received by payment mode, and the full receipt register |
| **Tally books** | The Society's Tally ledgers from 2018-19 to September 2026, as taken over from the accountant's computer |
| **Year closing** | Committee: freeze a year after audit, so nothing dated in it can be changed; reopen only with a reason |

For years before 2018-19 there were no individual bills or receipts in the system, so *Billing by head* and *Collections* aren't available; the audited figures are used instead.

Expense reports are in **Expenses** → **Monthly report** (section 12).

### Documents

The Society's papers: audited accounts, AGM minutes, important notices. Approved members can read them on their account page; the accounts team sees them here.

To add one (committee): **Add a document** → choose the **PDF file** (up to 15 MB; compress large scans first), give a **Title**, **Category** and the **Financial year** if it belongs to one (written like *2011-12*), an optional **Note**, then **Upload**. A document with a financial year is linked from that year's report.

### Old receipts from the past committee (2011 to 2016)

Each unit file has **Old receipts 2011–2016**, taken from the past committee's Excel sheets. When checking them against the physical receipt books, use **Check / edit** to record whether a line matches, correct it, or mark it as a duplicate or cancelled, always with a reason. Use **Add a receipt from the book** for receipts missing from the sheets. Every change is kept in the unit's *Change log*; old receipts can't be deleted. Needs *Add corrections* (the Accountant has it by default).

## 11. Members tab: approving members and sign-in history

The **Members** tab is where requests from owners, family, tenants and caretakers are approved or rejected, and where every sign-in attempt is recorded. It needs *Approve or remove member logins*.

### Requests waiting

Each request shows unit, name, a label for who they are (Owner, Tenant…), a *Google* label if they sign in with Google, mobile, email and date. If someone is already approved for that unit, a warning names them and their relation.

Before approving, check the person against the member register (I & J register) or ownership documents (Index II / agreement), or the rent agreement for a tenant.

- **Approve**: access starts at once, with the suggested level: unit account for owners and family, documents only for tenants and caretakers.
- **Reject**: a form asks for a **Reason** (the person sees it) and a **Note for the history** (only the committee sees it).
- **Edit**: correct name, unit, mobile, relation or access level before or after deciding.

### Approved members

The list shows each approved person with what they **Can see**. **Edit** changes their details or access (for example a tenant who pays the maintenance can be given the unit account, with the owner's consent). **Remove access** asks for a reason and moves them to the list below.

### Telling the person

After **Approve**, **Reject** or **Remove access**, a blue box offers **Send email** and **Send WhatsApp**. Each opens your own email or WhatsApp with a ready message: the approval with the sign-in link, or the decision with the reason. Check it and press send. The website doesn't send messages by itself. Opening one is recorded in the history as *Member told*.

### Rejected and removed

Everyone ever rejected or removed stays here, with the decision date, who decided and the reason. From here:

- **Open**: see the full history (sent, approved, rejected, edited, told), correct details or change the status;
- **Back to waiting** or **Approve** if the decision was a mistake;
- **Export CSV** for the records;
- **Delete permanently** (committee only, inside **Open**): only for test or mistaken records. It removes the record and its history and can't be undone. The person's Google or email login itself is removed separately by the person managing the database, if needed.

### Sign-in history

Every attempt to sign in, register or reset a password, on the website and in the app, is listed, including failed and closed ones. Passwords are never recorded.

- **Filters:** **Period** (7, 30, 90 days or everything), **Result** (failed, closed, signed in), **How** (Google, email and password, registration, password reset), **Where** (website or app).
- **Counts:** attempts, people or devices, signed in, failed, closed, and *still not in* (had a problem and no successful sign-in in that period). Phone those people.
- **Red boxes** list the most frequent problems in plain words with what to do, for example that the app needs its fingerprint added (section 16).
- **Export CSV**; the committee can tick test entries and **Delete selected**.

## 12. Expenses: bills, approvals and payments

Every rupee the Society spends is entered once with a photo of its bill, approved by someone other than the person who entered it, and linked to the payment that settled it. The bill photos are kept privately in the Society's own Google Drive, filed by financial year and month.

A rejected expense stops there. An approved one is settled in one of three ways depending on who paid, and every payment shows up under **Payments made** with its vouchers.

### Who does what

| Who | Usually |
| --- | --- |
| Watchman, housekeeping, manager, office assistant | Add expenses they spent or saw, with the bill photo |
| Accountant | Adds expenses, records payments to vendors, pays staff back, gives advances |
| Committee (Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer) | Approve or reject; never their own |
| Auditor, government official | See everything, change nothing |

The tab has these parts: **Add expense**, **Expenses**, **Staff paybacks**, **Payments made**, **Monthly report** and (committee) **Set-up**. Staff who can only add expenses see the first three.

### Adding one expense

1. **Expenses** → **Add expense** → **The bill is for: One expense**.
2. Enter **Date spent**, **Amount**, **Head** (e.g. Plumbing, Electricity, Housekeeping and cleaning, Lift maintenance and repair), **Paid to**, the **Bill / invoice no.** if any, and **What for**.
3. Under **Who paid?** choose one:
   - **The Society** (cash box, bank, UPI): choose how, and enter the reference (UPI, transaction or cheque number) unless it was cash;
   - **A person from their own money**: enter who paid. The Society pays them back later;
   - **Not paid yet**: a vendor's bill to be paid later.
4. **Add bill photo or scan**: take a photo with the phone camera, or choose a PDF. Up to 10 files. If there is no bill, photograph a handwritten note signed by the person who was paid.
5. **Save expense**. It now waits for approval.

Until it is approved, the person who added it can open it and **Correct** or **Delete** it.

### Several items on one sheet

Use this when someone hands in one sheet listing several small expenses, such as the watchman's list for the month.

1. **Add expense** → **The bill is for: Several items on one sheet**.
2. Fill one line per item: **Date**, **Head**, **What for**, **Paid to** (optional), **Amount**. **+ Add a line** for more. The total shows below.
3. Fill **Who paid?** once for the whole sheet (usually *A person from their own money* and the watchman's name).
4. Attach one photo of the sheet and **Save expense**.

Each line becomes its own expense with its own date and head, and all share the one photo. Example: the watchman's sheet of five items totalling ₹9,550 becomes five expenses.

### Approving or rejecting

Open an expense from the **Expenses** list (filter by **Status: Waiting for approval**). Look at the bill photo, then:

- **Approve**: it gets a voucher number such as *EXP/2026-27/0007*, and the photo moves to the month's *Approved* folder;
- **Reject**: write the reason (the person who added it sees it), then press **Reject** again.

For a sheet, the window lists all its lines; **Approve all … waiting on this sheet** approves them together with consecutive voucher numbers. You can't approve an expense you added yourself: the window says so, and another approver must do it.

### Paying a vendor later

For an approved *Not paid yet* expense, when the Society pays: open it → **Record payment** → enter **Paid on**, **How** and **Reference**, attach a photo of the cheque or the transfer as **Proof** if you have one, then press **Record payment** again.

### Paying staff back, and advances

**Staff paybacks** shows, for each person who paid from their own money: approved bills not yet paid back, any advance they hold, and the net amount due to them.

- **Pay back**: the bills are listed and ticked; untick any not being paid now. If they hold an advance, part of it can be set against the bills. Enter the date, how (e.g. Cheque) and the reference (e.g. the cheque number), attach a photo of the cheque as **Proof of payment**, then **Save**. One cheque can settle many bills: they are all marked *paid back* and linked to it (number like *PB/2026-27/0001*).
- **Give advance**: money given to staff before they spend it (petty cash), e.g. ₹2,000 to the watchman. It shows as *with them* until set against later bills.

### Payments made

Choose a month to see every payment that went out for expenses: vendors paid by the Society, paybacks and advances, with mode and reference, each linked to its vouchers. A 📎 marks vouchers with bill photos; tap one to open the expense with its bill.

### Monthly report

Choose the month to see: approved total; paid by the Society, by staff from their own money, and not yet paid; waiting and rejected; totals by head; the list of vouchers; payments made; and what is still owed to staff at month end. **Print / save PDF**, **Export CSV**, or **Save to Society records** (keeps a copy in the Drive's Reports folder).

### Lift costs recovered from members

For an approved expense about a lift (for example the C wing lift AMC of ₹14,000), the expense window shows **Recover from members' bills**:

1. Choose the **Lift** (C, D or both), the **Type** (e.g. AMC), the first bill month, and the number of monthly instalments (e.g. 12).
2. Check the line that shows how many flats share it and each flat's share.
3. **Add to members' bills**. It is added to those flats' maintenance bills (section 9), and the expense shows *Recovered from*.

Paying the vendor is still done with **Record payment**.

### Where the bill photos are kept

In the Society's Google account, in *Sunshree Suntec Society Records* → *Expenses* → financial year → month → *Submitted*, *Approved*, *Rejected* or *Payments*. Approved photos are named with their voucher number. Nobody outside can open them; on the accounts page, a photo shows only to people allowed to see that expense. The committee connects the storage once under **Set-up** (instructions are with the technical files).

## 13. Directory: for the watchman, housekeeping and manager

The **Directory** tab helps the gate and the office reach the right resident for a visitor, a delivery or an emergency. It needs *Residents directory*.

- Each unit is listed with the name in the Society's records and the people approved on the website: their name, who they are (Owner, Tenant…) and their mobile number.
- Tap a mobile number to call it.
- Type in **Search** to find a unit, a name or a number.
- It shows nothing about money.

This information is confidential. Don't copy it, photograph it, forward it or give numbers to visitors: call the resident yourself. Using it any other way breaks the staff agreement.

## 14. Website, Staff, Settings and My profile

### Website (publish notices)

The dues on the public website update by themselves. This tab is for what people type:

- **Notices**: enter the **Date**, **Title**, a **Short summary** (shown on the home page) and the **Full text** (leave a blank line between paragraphs), then **Publish notice**.
- **Committee and contact**: each committee member's role, name and phone, the **Society contact number** (also printed on bills) and **Office hours**, then **Save contact details**.

Publish only what the committee has approved.

### Staff (committee only)

**Requests from staff and officials.** People who registered as staff or officials (section 3) wait here.

- **Approve** opens a form filled from that role's usual access: permissions, whether the staff agreement is required, and an **Access until** date for auditors and officials. Change anything needed, then confirm. They can then sign in.
- **Reject** asks for a reason, which they see. Rejected requests stay listed, with **Back to waiting** and **Delete**.
- After either, **Send email** / **Send WhatsApp** opens a ready message to them.

**Add staff (invitation).** Instead of waiting for a request, the committee can invite someone by their Gmail address with a role and permissions. The invitation is used when they first sign in with that address. **Invitations not yet used** lists open invitations.

**Committee and staff list.** Each person with their role, permissions, agreement status and whether their access is active, suspended or ends on a date. **Edit** changes the role, any single permission, *Must accept the staff agreement*, *Access until*, or suspends access (untick *Access active*). **Remove access** ends it.

**Access by role.** For each role (Manager, Accountant, Office Assistant, Housekeeping, Watchman / Security, Auditor, Government official, Other): the permissions it gets, whether the agreement is required, and how many days access lasts (0 = until removed). **Save access by role**. This applies to new approvals and invitations; people who already have access are changed with **Edit**.

**The staff agreement.** Under **Agreement** the committee can edit the text (common points and duties per role) and **Publish as new version**; the **Acceptance record** shows who accepted which version and when. Everyone it applies to accepts it again at their next sign-in.

### Settings (committee only)

- **How bills are calculated**: the charge basis (the AGM 2015 method, or the Monthly Maintenance Calculation under the bye-laws once the General Body adopts it), monthly common expenses, water expense, construction cost per sq ft, property tax and insurance, parking rates, the yearly lift charge and penal interest. Always use **Preview** to check every unit's next bill before saving, and **Download CSV** to keep a copy.
- **Rates and due date**: the AGM 2015 rates for each charge category, **Days until due date**, and penal interest (at most 12% simple a year; 0 means none). The Society's contact details on bills are set in **Website**.
- One-time setup tools, used when the accounts were started: importing opening balances, the audited accounts 2011-12 to 2017-18, the past committee's receipts, and the Tally history. Don't run these again unless told to.

### My profile (everyone)

Shows your name, role, the permissions you have, any end date of your access, and the agreement you accepted. Ask the committee if something you need is missing.

## 15. The Android app

The app (icon: the Society's blue seal) shows the same website, member area and accounts page, and is easier on a phone: Google sign-in with the phone's own account, information readable without internet, and bill photos straight from the camera. It needs Android 8 or newer.

### Installing

1. Open the app file (*sunshreesuntec-…-release.apk*) that the committee shares on WhatsApp or Google Drive.
2. If the phone asks, allow installing apps from that source (*Install unknown apps* for WhatsApp, Files or Drive), then **Install**.
3. Open **Sunshree Suntec** from the home screen.

For a new version, install the new file the same way; your sign-in stays. If Android says the app *conflicts with an existing package*, uninstall the old one first and sign in again.

### Signing in

Tap **Member login** → **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account; the message under the button shows the progress. Email and password also work. If Google sign-in closes by itself, use email and password for now and tell the committee (section 16).

### Without internet

While online, the app saves your account, the Society's accounts and documents on the phone. Without internet it shows them with a yellow bar: *You're offline. Showing information saved on this phone on (date and time).* Nothing can be changed offline (no payments, approvals or expenses); the app says so and you can try again once connected.

### Using it

- **Pull down** on a page to refresh it.
- The phone's **Back** button goes to the previous page.
- Bills, receipts and documents open in the phone's PDF viewer, where you can save or share them; **Print** uses the phone's printing.
- **Bill photos:** when adding an expense, **Add bill photo or scan** offers *Choose a file or take a photo*; the camera opens and the photo goes straight into the form.
- Links to other websites, email and WhatsApp open in the phone's own apps.

**Sign out** on a phone you share or are giving away: it also removes the pages and files saved on that phone.

### Accounts

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How was my bill calculated? | Open the bill: each line has a note, and the last line names the resolution or bye-law used |
| A payment I made isn't shown | Bring the receipt or bank proof to the office; the committee records it or adds a correction |
| Why do only some flats pay lift charges? | Lift costs are shared only by flats that lift serves. Shops and A wing have no lift |
| Is interest charged on interest? | No: simple interest on unpaid principal only, at most 12% a year, and only if the General Body fixed a rate |
| What is BPM? | Builder-period pending maintenance, shown separately and not added to your balance |
| My cheque bounced | The receipt is cancelled (kept on record), the amount returns to your account, and cheque return charges may be added as fixed by the General Body |
| Who can see my mobile number and email? | The committee, staff allowed to approve members, and gate / office staff with the residents directory (mobile only), all under a confidentiality agreement. The public page shows only names and amounts |
| Who changed my account? | Every entry records who made it and when, and nothing is deleted; ask the committee |

### Expenses

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
