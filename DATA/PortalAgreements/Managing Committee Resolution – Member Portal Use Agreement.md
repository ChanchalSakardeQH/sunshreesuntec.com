**Agenda item no. \[●\]:** To consider and approve the Member Portal Use Agreement and Undertaking (Version 1.0) for the Society’s online member portal at www.sunshreesuntec.com, and the safeguards for running the portal.

## Background

The Society runs an online member portal at www.sunshreesuntec.com. Members can see their maintenance bills, receipts, account information, Society notices and unit-related records there, and upload their unit’s documents.

The Managing Committee has considered the draft Member Portal Use Agreement and Undertaking. It sets out the terms of portal access and use, members’ responsibilities, payment and financial-record procedures, document submission with physical acknowledgment, grievances, responsible communication, personal data protection, access control and record management.

The Committee recognises that the portal is a supplementary administrative facility. It does not replace statutory records, books of account, legally prescribed notices, members’ inspection rights or remedies available under applicable law.

## Resolution

“**RESOLVED THAT** the Managing Committee of Sunshree Suntec Co-operative Housing Society Ltd., having considered the draft *Member Portal Use Agreement and Undertaking – Version 1.0*, approves it in principle, subject to review and vetting by the Society’s appointed advocate and the incorporation of any legally necessary modifications.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Agreement shall govern the use of the Society’s online member portal by members and other persons duly authorised by the Society, subject to the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960, the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Rules, 1961, the Society’s registered bye-laws and lawful resolutions of the competent Society authorities.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the portal shall operate as a supplementary digital facility, and shall not replace or override the Society’s statutory registers, original supporting records, books of account, audited financial statements, prescribed modes of service of notices, or the statutory rights and remedies available to members.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** from the effective date of the final Agreement, every approved portal user, including those already using the portal, shall be asked to accept it electronically before continuing to use the portal; the accepted copy shall be emailed to the user with a copy to the Society’s official email address and kept in the Society’s records for the unit.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** members who do not accept or use the portal shall continue to be given reasonable alternative means to obtain bills, receipts, notices and information and to access records, in accordance with applicable law and the registered bye-laws; not using the portal shall not, by itself, affect their membership or statutory rights.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** documents submitted by members (title documents with Index II, leave-and-licence agreements, police intimation, occupant details and applications) shall be acknowledged in writing on receipt of the physical copy, with a serial acknowledgment number recorded on the portal, and the original records shall be kept in the Society’s office.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Society shall maintain appropriate administrative and technical safeguards for the portal, including, as far as practicable:

- role-based, least-privilege access, with access for committee members and staff granted and withdrawn by Managing Committee decision;
- strong authentication, and multi-factor authentication for accounts with administrative access;
- encryption of data in transit and appropriate protection of stored data;
- audit logs of important administrative and record-related activity;
- periodic access reviews and prompt removal of obsolete accounts;
- security updates and remediation of known vulnerabilities;
- regular backups of essential records under the Society’s own control, with a tested restore procedure; and
- a procedure for responding to suspected security incidents, preserving evidence and making notifications required by law.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Society’s domain, portal administration, official records, member database and administrative credentials shall remain under the control of the Society, held in accounts owned by the Society, and shall be handed over securely on any change in the Managing Committee.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** any developer, hosting provider or other service provider engaged for the portal shall be given access only to the extent necessary for its assigned work, under written terms covering confidentiality, security, permitted use, access restrictions, incident reporting, subcontracting, and return or deletion of data at the end of the engagement; no service provider shall acquire ownership of Society records or any right to use member data for its own purposes; and the Society shall ensure that essential records remain accessible to it if a service provider or arrangement changes or ends.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Society shall prepare and publish a separate Member Privacy Notice, and portal access and records-management procedures, consistent with applicable data-protection and other laws, before the Agreement is published for acceptance.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Secretary and/or such other office-bearer as the Managing Committee may designate is authorised to:

1. Send the Agreement to the Society’s advocate for legal review and final vetting.
2. Incorporate changes recommended by the advocate, provided they do not exceed the Managing Committee’s authority or conflict with applicable law and the registered bye-laws.
3. Prepare the Member Privacy Notice for approval.
4. Place the vetted Agreement and Privacy Notice before the next Managing Committee meeting for final adoption and fixing of the effective date.
5. Place the Agreement before the next General Body meeting for information and, where required under applicable law or the registered bye-laws, approval.
6. Arrange publication of the approved documents on the portal and inform members.
7. Keep records of acceptance, including the Agreement version and the date and time of acceptance.
8. Coordinate with the portal developer and any service provider to put in place the safeguards in this resolution.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the final vetted Agreement shall be placed on record and made available to members before or when their electronic acceptance is sought, and any material change to it, or any matter beyond the Managing Committee’s authority, shall be placed before the competent Society authority as required under applicable law and the registered bye-laws.”
