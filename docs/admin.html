Interest on past arrears

Works out simple interest on each unit’s overdue balance, month by month, from the ledger (the old Tally books and these accounts), at the rate in force each month under Settings → Penal interest rate periods. It is the same way a monthly bill charges interest: the balance at the start of the month, less interest already billed, × the month’s rate ÷ 12. Months whose bill already charged interest are left out. Nothing is charged until you add it to a month’s bills; it can be cancelled until those bills are made.

Charge interest only from the date of the General Body resolution that fixed the rate, and tell members before the bills go out. Members see the calculation for their unit, the rate periods and the reference documents on their account page.

              Settings

              Start the accounts

              Creates all 65 units with their balance as on 30 September 2026 from the society statement, and posts the October 2026 bill (arrears plus ₹6,500 other charges) exactly as it was sent to members. This runs once.

              Audited accounts 2011-12 to 2017-18

              Load the figures read from the audit reports (balance sheets, income & expenditure, members’ year-end dues, builder pending maintenance, list of members, committee) from audited-accounts-2011-2018.json. Keep that file private. Then add the scanned reports themselves under Documents.

              Past committee receipts (October 2011 – October 2016)

              Load the receipts from the past committee’s Excel sheets into each unit’s ledger, from past-receipts-2011-2016.json (keep it private). Loading again refreshes the lines nobody has checked yet; lines already checked or corrected are kept. Load the audited accounts first, so the ledger can balance each year to the audited figure.

              Unit files in Google Drive: backup and restore

              Each unit has a folder in the Society’s Google Drive (Sunshree Suntec Society Records → Units → wing → unit) holding its scans (members and shares, tenants and agreements, communication, electricity, property tax) and “unit file backup.json”: everything about the unit, including details, member and tenant records, communication, electricity and tax records, ledger, bills, receipts and old receipts. The backup is updated by itself whenever a unit record is saved. Back up all units at least once a month, and before any big change.

              Restore a unit’s records from its backup

              Puts back member, tenant, communication, electricity and property tax records that are in the Drive backup but missing here (for example, deleted by mistake). Records that exist here are not changed. Bills, receipts and the ledger are permanent records and can’t be rewritten from the website; their copies stay in the backup.

              Tally migration

              Bring the Society’s Tally books into these accounts. Use the export file made on the accountant’s laptop by Export-TallyData.bat (tally-export-….json), or the earlier tally-ledgers-2018-2026.json. Keep these files private; they are read here in your browser and saved only to the Society’s database.

              Member ledgers → units

              Each member’s Tally ledger is matched to a unit by the unit number at the start of its name. Check the matches; choose the unit for any that are blank, or leave blank to skip.

              Charge categories and rates (AGM 2015 method)

              The rates passed at the AGM held on 25/01/2015 (minutes dated 25/02/2015), effective 1 April 2015 and unchanged since. Monthly amounts per unit. Lift is per year, or per month where a monthly amount is given (flats used as commercial: ₹500 a month). Where ₹ per sq ft is given (D wing: ₹3), maintenance is that rate × the unit’s carpet area. Units without a recorded carpet area pay the fixed amount until it is entered (Units → edit). Change these when the General Body revises them; new bills use the new rates.

              Penal interest on arrears: rate periods

              Simple interest per year, at the rate fixed by the General Body. Each rate applies from its date until the next one starts. Ceiling: 21% before 18/07/2026; 12% from 18/07/2026 (MCS (Amendment) Rules, 2026, Rule 106C-12(4)-6). Bills for a month that spans two rates are charged by days.
