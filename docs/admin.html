Lift expenses

Record each lift expense other than the yearly lift charge (AMC invoice, repair, wear and tear, parts, licence). It is divided equally among the units served by that lift and added to their bills as “Lift AMC / Repairs (shared)”, in one month or spread over several months. Each bill lists the expense it includes.
