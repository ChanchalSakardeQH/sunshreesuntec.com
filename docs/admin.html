Lift expenses

Record each lift expense other than the yearly lift charge (AMC invoice, repair, wear and tear, parts, licence). It is divided equally among the units served by that lift and added to their bills as “Lift AMC / Repairs (shared)”, in one month or spread over several months. Each bill lists the expense it includes.

How bills are calculated

Switch to the Monthly Maintenance Calculation once the General Body adopts it. Use Preview to check every unit’s bill first.

1. Service charges: equal contribution

Monthly common expenses (Bye-law 66). “All units” items are divided equally among every unit; lift items only among the units served by that lift, so shops without a lift don’t pay them.
