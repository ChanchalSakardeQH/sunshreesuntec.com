    About the society

    Sunshree Suntec Co-operative Housing Society Ltd. is a registered co-operative housing society on NIBM Road, Kondhwa Khurd, Pune. The premises has residential flats, commercial offices, a hotel terrace, ground-floor shops and basement shops.

    How maintenance is billed

    Maintenance is billed to each unit holder as per the Society’s bye-laws and General Body resolutions. Balances on this website are taken from the Society’s accounts and are updated after each statement.

    What the columns mean
    Member outstanding
    The balance against the unit holder in the Society’s accounts today, including the latest bill and every payment recorded. “Cr” means the member has paid in advance.
    Builder pending maintenance (BPM)
    Maintenance from the builder period, kept separate from the member’s own balance.
    Under verification
    The opening balance for this unit is still being checked against the Society’s records.
