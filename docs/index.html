    How maintenance is billed

    Maintenance is billed to each unit holder as per the Society’s bye-laws and General Body resolutions. Balances on this website are taken from the Society’s accounts and are updated after each statement.

    What the columns mean
    Member outstanding
    The closing balance against the unit holder in the Society’s accounts. “Cr” means the Society owes the member (an advance or excess payment).
    Builder pending maintenance (BPM)
    Maintenance from the builder period, kept separate from the member’s own balance.
    Not listed
    No individual balance was found for this unit in the statement. It does not mean the unit has no dues.
